## 2. Information we collect

When you message our Page, we receive the following through the Meta Messenger Platform:

- **Page-scoped ID (PSID):** a unique identifier Meta assigns to you for our Page. It is not your Facebook account ID.
- **Message content:** the text, and any attachments or postback data, that you send us.
- **Message metadata:** timestamps and message identifiers.
- **Basic profile information:** such as your name, only if Meta provides it and our configuration requests it.
- **Our replies:** the responses the assistant sends back to you.

We do **not** receive your Facebook password, your friends list, or your private Facebook activity. We ask you not to send sensitive information (such as government ID numbers, payment card details, passwords, or health information) in chat.
